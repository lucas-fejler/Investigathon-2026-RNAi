# Bibliografía

## Herramientas computacionales

1. Lück S, Kreszies T, Strickert M, Schweizer P, Kuhlmann M, Douchkov D. (2019).
   *siRNA-Finder (si-Fi) Software for RNAi-Target Design and Off-Target Prediction*.
   Frontiers in Plant Science, 10:1023. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2019.01023).
2. SIREN. *Suite for Intelligent RNAi design and Evaluation of Nucleotide sequences*.
   [Código y documentación](https://github.com/pablovargasmejia/SIREN).
3. Chen Y et al. (2025). *dsRNAEngineer: a web-based tool of comprehensive dsRNA
   design for pest control*. Trends in Biotechnology.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tibtech.2025.01.002).

## Antecedentes de selección de genes

4. Andrada NL et al. (2026). *ETH and Burs-α are necessary for the normal molting
   in Dalbulus maidis and Delphacodes kuscheli*. Frontiers in Insect Science,
   6:1811933. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/finsc.2026.1811933).
5. Galetto L et al. (2021). *Silencing of ATP synthase β induces female sterility
   in a leafhopper phytoplasma vector*. Entomologia Generalis.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1127/entomologia/2021/1267).
6. Rossi M et al. (2024). *Lab-scale method for plant-mediated delivery of dsRNAs
   to phloem-feeding leafhoppers*. Journal of Pest Science.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10340-023-01670-0).
7. Canuto F et al. (2025). *RNAi-mediated silencing of a regulatory subunit of the
   26S proteasome induces mortality and female sterility in leafhopper vectors
   of grapevine Flavescence dorée phytoplasmas*. Journal of Pest Science.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10340-025-01961-8).
8. Jones TKL et al. (2021). *Assessing the Functionality of RNA Interference
   (RNAi) in the Phloem-feeding Maize pest Dalbulus maidis*. bioRxiv, preprint.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1101/2021.09.29.462424).
